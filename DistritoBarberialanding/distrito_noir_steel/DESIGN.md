## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Mood
This design system embodies the ritual, poise, and sharp execution of high-end traditional grooming. Rooted in an artisanal yet unapologetically metropolitan spirit, the interface conveys exclusivity, masculine discipline, tactile heritage, and precision. It creates the visceral feeling of stepping into an exclusive private club: aged leather, warm straight-razors, cold steel blades, and low atmospheric ambient lighting.

### Target Audience
Men seeking refined grooming rituals, precision fade and beard craft, luxury hair care, and a frictionless digital booking experience in Hermosillo, Sonora.

### Aesthetic Movement
**Dark Editorial Brutalism & Monochromatic Minimalism**. The aesthetic relies on strict geometric discipline, sharp corners, pitch-black cavernous depth, high-contrast monochrome typography, and razor-thin metallic hairline borders. It deliberately avoids decorative gradients and playful roundings in favor of raw editorial poise and tailored architectural grids.

## Colors

The palette is strictly anchored in extreme tonal contrast, reflecting the monochrome legacy of traditional barber insignia and chrome-plated tools.

- **Background & Canvas (`#0B0B0B`, `#121212`, `#18181B`)**: Pitch-black foundation that reduces visual glare and elevates typography and service photography.
- **Primary Text & High Accent (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure razor white used for high-impact headlines, primary CTAs, and active states.
- **Secondary Chrome & Silver (`#D1D5DB`, `#9CA3AF`)**: Metallic muted grays for secondary copy, subtle icons, and unselected states, mimicking brushed stainless steel shears.
- **Tertiary Amber Gold (`#C59B27`)**: An ultra-restrained warm accent reminiscent of single-malt whiskey and aged barber chair leather. Reserved exclusively for VIP badges, ritual tags, and precision booking confirmations.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#27272A`, `#3F3F46`)**: Subdued graphite lines that enforce structural precision without clashing with the dark background.

## Typography

The typographical pairing reflects the identity established in the brand mark: the high-contrast drama of a stately editorial serif (`Bodoni Moda`) joined with the surgical clarity of an architectural sans-serif (`Hanken Grotesk`).

- **Display & Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`)**: High stroke contrast, pronounced verticality, and sculpted serifs. Headers should frequently be styled in uppercase with generous letter spacing (`0.04em` to `0.12em`), channeling the aesthetic of prestige heritage publications and high-end tailoring.
- **Body & Metadata (`Hanken Grotesk`)**: Clean, low-distortion grotesk delivering neutral, effortless legibility across service descriptions, booking timetables, and pricing tiers.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Rendered in uppercase with pronounced tracking (`0.14em`–`0.18em`) to mirror luxury product packaging and artisan credentials.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Model
A structured 12-column grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to 4 columns on mobile. Generous vertical pauses between sections mirror the unhurried atmosphere of an exclusive grooming salon.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Adaptations
- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: Max container width of `1280px`. Outer margins set to `2rem` (`32px`), column gutters set to `1.5rem` (`24px`). Asymmetrical multi-column card splits allow hero service features to claim dominant visual real estate.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout. Margin reduced to `1.5rem` (`24px`), gutter to `1rem` (`16px`). Service menus transition to 2-column stacked lists.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4-column layout. Margin clamped to `1rem` (`16px`), gutter to `0.75rem` (`12px`). Booking flow, barber selection, and service cards stack into an uninterrupted vertical linear progression.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids soft, floating cloud shadows in favor of **structural layering and razor-thin metallic boundaries**:

- **Tonal Stepping**: Depth is established by stepping surface tones rather than elevation offsets:
  - Base canvas: `#0B0B0B`
  - Container / Card background: `#121212`
  - Elevated Popovers / Drawers / Overlays: `#18181B`
- **Hairline Outlines**: Visual separation uses 1px solid borders in `#27272A` (default) and `#3F3F46` (hover/focus).
- **Subtle Dark Ambient Occlusion**: For elevated modals, a crisp black scrim (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`) is coupled with a sharp, low-spread drop shadow: `0 16px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.9)`.
- **Specular Metallic Accents**: Active or hovered cards introduce a subtle metallic perimeter highlight (`1px solid #D1D5DB`), creating the cold tactile edge of polished steel shears.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a strictly **Sharp (0px)** philosophy.

- Corners of cards, buttons, dialogs, badges, and image masks are unrounded (`0px border-radius`).
- This angular architectural geometry complements the sharp edges of classic razor fades, straight-edge barbering, and the chiseled Blackletter geometry of the monogram.
- Circular elements are prohibited, with the sole exception of client and master barber avatar portraits when displayed in compact circular crops.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Solid white background (`#FFFFFF`), pure black text (`#0B0B0B`), zero corner radius, bold uppercase font, `0.14em` letter spacing. On hover: inverts to high-sheen silver (`#E5E7EB`) with immediate 150ms transition.
- **Secondary CTA (Outline)**: 1px border in `#3F3F46`, transparent background, pure white text. On hover: border transitions to `#FFFFFF`, background to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Tertiary / Ritual CTA**: Bordered in muted amber (`#C59B27`) with gold-tinted hover states for VIP reservations and exclusive packages.

### Service Cards
- Constructed with `#121212` fill and `#27272A` hairline border.
- Features a two-part lockup: large editorial serif title with monospace-aligned pricing (`$ MXN`), followed by duration and ritual breakdown in secondary sans-serif.
- Hover state raises border contrast to `#D1D5DB` without vertical translation.

### Chips & Badges
- Sharp rectangular capsules (`0px radius`).
- Background `#18181B`, hairline border `#27272A`, small uppercase text (`label-sm`).
- Selected status switches to white fill with black text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: `#0E0E10` with 1px bottom border or all-around hairline border `#27272A`.
- Active focus state: crisp `#FFFFFF` border, no glow rings.
- Text entry in `#FFFFFF`, placeholder text in `#71717A`.

### Lists & Appointment Slots
- Bordered horizontal dividers (`1px solid #27272A`) separating calendar intervals and artisan profiles.
- Barber selection cards incorporate high-contrast black-and-white portraits with editorial credentials and signature services.
